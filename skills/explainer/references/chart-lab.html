STEP 1 OF 5
One context window
A context window holds 200,000 tokens. On this stage, each dot is one token. Click the chart, then scroll to fill it.
STEP 2 OF 5
Every turn starts the same
The system prompt and the tool definitions load first: 36,000 tokens, in every turn.
STEP 3 OF 5
History grows
Each message and tool result adds to the history. Here it reaches 128,000 tokens. Only 36,000 are free.
STEP 4 OF 5
Compaction
Near the limit, compaction folds the old history into a short summary. Watch the old history fly into the summary block.
STEP 5 OF 5
The same window, as a bar
The dots become a capacity bar. After compaction, 131,000 tokens are free for the next turns.
